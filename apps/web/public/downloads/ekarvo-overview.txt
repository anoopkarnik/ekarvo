Ekarvo is building a connected path from operational understanding to controlled action and evidence.

EKARVO PULSE · OPERATING INTELLIGENCE
Pulse is designed to connect demand, capacity, performance, quality and economics so teams can understand the effects of a decision before changing the work.

Proposed capability groups: Plan (Forecast, Capacity, Allocate, Simulate); Operate (Command, Perform, Quality, Economics); Develop (Academy, Coach); Optimize (Discover, Optimize).

EKARVO DRIVE · AUTONOMOUS EXECUTION
Drive is designed to carry defined work through approved steps, visible exceptions and checks against the agreed result.

Proposed capability groups: Design (Studio, Agents, Knowledge); Execute (Voice, Browser, Connect, Document, Interact, Desktop); Orchestrate (Flow, Context, Control); Assure (Verify, Observe); Deploy (Runtime).

ONE CONNECTED MODEL
Detect → Understand → Simulate → Authorize → Execute → Verify → Learn. People retain authority for decisions and exceptions. Evidence stays connected to the result.
