EKARVO connects operational signals with verified outcomes through seven stages: detect, understand, simulate, authorize, execute, verify, and learn.

## Explore a scenario

The [simulation studio](/landing#scene-08) demonstrates service and case operations, supply and order coordination, and financial shared services. Choose an operational function, inspect the workflow, and review its linked evidence.

## Authority and evidence

Policy conditions identify permitted actions and actions requiring approval. The evidence trail explains the objective, authorized action, actual result, and verification outcome at each step.

All scenarios on the landing page are interactive demonstrations. They do not perform actions in external enterprise systems. To discuss a real workflow, use **Talk to EKARVO** on the [landing page](/landing).
