Pulse is EKARVO’s operating intelligence layer. It connects planning, operations, workforce development, and continuous improvement.

## Plan

Forecast demand, model capacity, allocate work, and compare interventions before committing resources.

## Operate

Review queue exposure, throughput, quality, and operating economics together.

## Develop

Identify skill gaps and explore coaching and learning pathways.

## Optimize

Identify repeatable work suitable for Drive and pass an operational recommendation into the execution experience.

The [Pulse demo](/landing#scene-06) uses illustrative data to show how capacity interventions affect projected backlog and service exposure.
